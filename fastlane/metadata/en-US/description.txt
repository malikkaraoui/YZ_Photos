YZPhotos sorts your photos and videos right where they actually live: on your external USB-C drive or your SMB network share. No import, no copy into the app, no cloud. You keep full control of your files.

Plug in your SSD, pick a folder, and flip through your photos one by one like a deck of cards. One swipe to keep, one swipe to send to the trash. What you delete goes to a trash folder on the drive, never erased without your approval.

What you can do:
Sort your photos and videos by swiping
Spot exact duplicates and near identical shots
Find screenshots to delete in bulk
Browse your folders and Apple photo libraries
See how much space you will free up in real time
Rotate a photo and save it right away
Zoom in and view full screen

Simple and safe:
Nothing is moved or deleted without you
The trash lives on the drive, you can restore anything
Works offline, no data collected
Works with USB-C drives and SMB network shares
Made for iPhone and iPad

Paid app, no ads.
